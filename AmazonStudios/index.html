Storytelling Sensibility

What I care about bringing to screen

The stories I am most drawn to are the ones that sit at the intersection of three things: diaspora narratives and culturally diverse perspectives that rarely get centered in mainstream content; music and sound that shape emotional experience rather than just accompany it; and underdog stories about people who achieved something great against the odds.

Those three things are connected for me. The music that moves me most tends to come from people who had to fight for the right to make it. The stories I find most compelling tend to follow people navigating between worlds -- cultural, generational, economic -- and finding something true on the other side. Animation is one of the few formats where all of that can coexist without compromise, where the visual and the emotional and the sonic can do exactly what they need to do in the same frame.

That's why I want to be in this room specifically, not just in a coordination role generally.
