01

On Documentary Storytelling

What Brought Me Here

Henry Louis Gates Jr.'s Find Your Roots is the reason I started building my family tree. Watching that series, I became captivated by how documentary storytelling could take something as intimate as a person's ancestry and transform it into something universal. That curiosity sent me down my own path, reaching out to elders in my family, piecing together stories that had never been written down, and realizing that the most powerful narratives are the ones built on rigorous research and genuine human curiosity. That relationship between deep research, human story, and documentary craft is what draws me to National Geographic's work.
