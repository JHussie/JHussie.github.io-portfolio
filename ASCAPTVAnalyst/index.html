Why ASCAP

On ASCAP's Mission

What I find particularly compelling about ASCAP's mission is the not-for-profit structure, the idea that nearly 90 cents of every dollar collected goes back to music creators. In a music industry where some have to choose between true expression and trends, or art appreciation and exploitation, I admire ASCAP's commitment to being supportive of composers and songwriters as they get the recognition and compensation they deserve. The TV Analyst role is an operations position, but it exists in service of something meaningful: ensuring that the people who make music are accurately credited and fairly paid. That's the kind of work I want to build a career in.
