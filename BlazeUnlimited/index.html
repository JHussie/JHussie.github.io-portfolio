On Sync Placement

A Placement I Have Not Stopped Thinking About

"Hunt You Down" by Lee Richardson, Jonathan Murrill, Tom Ford, James Cocozza, and Raxstar is the closing theme of each episode of Netflix's 2026 K-drama Teach You a Lesson. On the surface it is a driving, confident track. What makes the placement work is how precisely it functions within the episode structure. After watching each episode, which tends to be chaotic, emotionally taxing, or morally complicated, the song recenters you on the ERPB's motivations. It does not summarize what you just watched. It recalibrates how you feel about it. That decision to use the closing theme as a psychological reset rather than just an aesthetic flourish is the kind of intentional supervision that makes a show more watchable across a full run.
