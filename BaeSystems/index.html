I'm Jabreneé Hussie, a communications and content strategy professional based in the DMV, with a background that spans enterprise intranet communications, strategic research reports, branded content development, and cross-functional content coordination. I hold a Master of Entertainment Industry Management from Carnegie Mellon University's Heinz College and a B.S. in Information Technology from Delaware State University, where I graduated Summa Cum Laude from the Honors Program.

How an organization communicates internally shapes how its people show up externally. I bring both the operational discipline and the storytelling craft to make that communication work.

At Inovalon I authored and published content for an enterprise SharePoint intranet serving 10,000+ employees in a regulated environment, used Microsoft Teams for executive presentations, client coordination, and division-wide communications, and built compliance documentation frameworks that kept complex information accessible and accurate across departments. At CMU I produced strategic research reports, branded content strategies, and leadership presentations on emerging technology topics including AI. Across multiple organizations I have coordinated cross-functional communications workflows, managed multiple simultaneous content pipelines, and delivered consistent written output on deadline.

I believe that the infrastructure behind great communication matters as much as the communication itself. Clear messages, the right channels, consistent tone, and measurable outcomes — that discipline is what I bring to every organization I work with, and it is exactly what the BAE Systems communications team is building.
