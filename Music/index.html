The Soundtrack
That Shapes Me

Raised in the DMV. Violin trained. Research-driven. I came to music supervision through the intersection of technology and storytelling — a speech emotion recognition thesis that revealed how deeply audio shapes human experience, and a sync licensing internship that showed me how that understanding translates into real industry decisions.

My work centers on music that helps people see themselves in stories — particularly those from the African diaspora, Black communities, and underrepresented cultures globally.
