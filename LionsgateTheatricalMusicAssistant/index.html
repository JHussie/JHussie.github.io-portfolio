Growing up in the early 2000s, I watched Step Up and every dance film I could find, and what I remember most isn't the choreography -- it's how the music made me feel like I was inside the movement. That's when I started understanding that music in film doesn't just accompany a story; it pulls the strings on our attention, our emotions, and our investment in the characters on screen. That fascination never left, and it's what eventually led me to music supervision. This page is a walk through the work that took me there.

Brief Response — Meridian (Working Title)

Creative Brief — Illustrative
Re: Feature Brief — "Meridian" (Working Title)
Mood playlist request, Act II

A preliminary mood playlist for a film's second act, centered on a series of late-night conversations between a 17-year-old girl of West African and Caribbean descent and her grandmother on a back porch. The scenes are slow, intimate, and emotionally layered -- the grandmother sharing stories about home that the protagonist has never heard before.

Music in these scenes should feel like memory, like distance, like something loved from far away. Genres welcome: Afrobeats, Afropop, soul, R&B, acoustic, neo-soul, Afro-folk, ambient with melodic elements. Lyrical or instrumental both welcome.
