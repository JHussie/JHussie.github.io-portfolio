On Soundtracks

Why this specifically

The music I return to most is music tied to a moment in a film or a scene in a show -- not because the music is better than other music, but because the combination of image and sound creates something neither could create alone. That relationship is what Milan Records exists to serve, and it is the part of the music industry I have been most drawn to throughout my career in sync licensing and music supervision.

My graduate capstone research at Carnegie Mellon examined how independent labels adapt to the evolving digital ecosystem -- including how DSPs, direct-to-fan strategies, and sync licensing shape how catalog music reaches audiences. That research gives me a working understanding of how a label like Milan Records fits into the broader music distribution landscape, not just as a catalog but as a business.
